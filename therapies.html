Four disciplines, one clinic

Chiropractic, physiotherapy, therapeutic massage and acupuncture are available under one roof. Our practitioners recommend the treatment most appropriate to your condition, and combine therapies where this supports a faster recovery.

Chiropractic

with Dr Jenny Donovan

The clinic's founding discipline. Precise, gentle care for the spine and joints, and the conditions they give rise to, including headaches, sciatica and postural pain. Led by Dr Jenny Donovan, with particular expertise in pregnancy and paediatric care.

About chiropractic →

Physiotherapy · Elements

with James Galea

Structured rehabilitation with James Galea of Elements Physiotherapy: sports injuries, joint and muscle conditions, and guided recovery before and after surgery, supported by a clear programme between sessions.

About physiotherapy →

Therapeutic Massage

with Tracy Ward

Targeted soft-tissue treatment for muscular tension, overuse and stress, together with aromatherapy and Indian head massage, provided by Tracy Ward.

About therapeutic massage →

Acupuncture

Cat Hill Clinic team

An evidence-informed option for persistent pain and general wellbeing, offered on its own or alongside chiropractic and massage as part of a coordinated treatment plan.
