Chiropractic

Gentle, precise care for the spine and joints, provided by Dr Jenny Donovan, Doctor of Chiropractic and Clinic Principal.

What chiropractic helps with

Chiropractic is concerned with the movement of the spine and joints, and the consequences when that movement is restricted. Such problems rarely remain local: restriction in the lower back may present as sciatica, and a stiff neck as recurring headaches. Chiropractic care addresses the underlying cause rather than the symptom alone.

  • Low back pain and sciatica
  • Neck pain, stiffness and tension headaches
  • Postural problems from desk work
  • Joint pain in hips, shoulders and knees
  • Care during pregnancy and for children — a particular interest of Dr Donovan's

Dr Donovan's approach

Dr Donovan uses a range of gentle techniques, including precise spinal adjustment, soft-tissue therapy and practical guidance on posture and exercise, each adapted to the patient's age, build and comfort. Treatment is tailored to the individual, whether an expectant mother, an athlete or a child.

Your first visit

Please allow approximately 45 minutes. Your first visit begins with a full case history and physical examination, followed by a clear explanation of the findings, the recommended treatment and the number of sessions likely to be required. If chiropractic is not the most appropriate treatment for your condition, we will advise you and refer you accordingly, often to a colleague within the clinic.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is chiropractic treatment painful?
Most patients describe an adjustment as a sensation of pressure followed by relief, rather than pain. Dr Donovan uses gentle, low-force techniques where appropriate and explains each step before proceeding.
What causes the clicking sound?
The sound is gas being released within the joint as it moves, similar to the sound of knuckles cracking. It is not bone moving against bone, and an adjustment can be fully effective without any sound at all.
How many sessions will I need?
This depends on the nature of the condition and how long it has been present. You will be given a realistic estimate at your first assessment, and the plan is reviewed as your treatment progresses.
Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy?
Yes. Pregnancy care is one of Dr Donovan's particular interests. Techniques are adapted to each stage of pregnancy and to your comfort.
