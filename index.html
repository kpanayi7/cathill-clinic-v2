Your first visit

Three simple steps to feeling better

  1. 1

    Message or call

    WhatsApp us or call 020 8275 0656. Our reception team replies the same working day and books you in with the right practitioner.

  2. 2

    A thorough assessment

    Your first appointment begins with a full history and examination, so we understand the cause of your pain, not just where it hurts.

  3. 3

    Your treatment plan

    You leave with a clear plan: what we will do, how many sessions to expect, and what to do at home between visits.
