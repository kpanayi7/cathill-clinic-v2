The Elements approach

Five elements of recovery

Getting better from an injury is more than treating the pain. Every plan covers the whole journey, so the problem is resolved and stays resolved.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis

    A thorough assessment to find what is really causing the problem, not only where it hurts.

  2. 2

    Treatment

    Hands-on therapy and targeted techniques to settle pain and restore movement.

  3. 3

    Rehabilitation

    A progressive exercise programme that rebuilds strength, control and confidence.

  4. 4

    Prevention

    Addressing the habits, loads and weak links that led to the injury.

  5. 5

    Education

    Clear explanations, so you understand your body and how to look after it.

Your physiotherapist

About James

James qualified in physiotherapy from the University of Hertfordshire in 2006 and has worked in the NHS, in private practice and with sports teams. He completed his acupuncture training with the Acupuncture Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in 2011.

Treatment begins with a thorough assessment and combines hands-on therapy, where it helps, with a progressive exercise programme designed to be followed at home and reviewed at each visit.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim through private health insurance?
Yes. Elements Physiotherapy works with most private health insurers and accepts consultant referrals. Please check your policy for any pre-authorisation requirements before your first appointment.
Do I need a scan or diagnosis first?
No. A physiotherapy assessment is usually the right first step, and in most cases imaging is not required. If a scan or specialist opinion would be helpful, James will advise you.
Will I need to do exercises at home?
In most cases, yes. A home programme is central to lasting recovery. It is designed to be realistic and is reviewed and adjusted at each visit.
