Established in East Barnet since 1997

Cat Hill Clinic was established as a chiropractic clinic in East Barnet in 1997 and has since grown to incorporate additional therapies, including physiotherapy, massage and acupuncture, providing our patients with an integrated approach to their care.

Our approach

Individual care, delivered to professional standards

A family practice

The clinic has established itself as a family practice, where we welcome people of all ages and abilities, with a wide variety of conditions, in a relaxed and friendly environment.

Patient-focused care

We strive to provide our patients with high standards of professional and ethical care. Your treatment plan is tailored to your individual needs, and with four therapies complementing each other, we will find the right path for your recovery and wellness journey.

Professional standards

Each of our experienced practitioners is registered and regulated by their professional body and association, providing high professional standards in practice.

Our practitioners

Meet the team

Jennifer Donovan BSc(Hons) DC

Doctor of Chiropractic · Clinic Principal

Jenny graduated with first-class honours from the Welsh Institute of Chiropractic at the University of Glamorgan in 2001 and joined Cat Hill Chiropractic Clinic in 2002. She took over as Principal in 2008 and has continued to develop and expand the therapies offered, making Cat Hill Clinic the multidisciplinary practice it is today. She particularly enjoys being part of the local community and has encouraged a welcoming, inclusive family feel to the practice.

She uses a range of techniques, including gentle spinal adjustments (Diversified, SOT, Activator and Drop technique), soft-tissue techniques and massage, and can offer advice on posture and exercise. She has vast experience in treating a wide range of problems in people of all ages, with a particular interest in chiropractic care for pregnant women and children. She enjoys attending CPD (continuing professional development) courses to bring up-to-date, evidence-based practice to her patients.

Jenny enjoys walking, spending time with family and friends, and trying new restaurants!

Registered member of the General Chiropractic Council, the British Chiropractic Association and the College of Chiropractors

James Galea BSc SRP AACP CSP

Physiotherapist · Elements Physiotherapy

James trained at the University of Hertfordshire, where he qualified as a physiotherapist following his first degree in sports rehabilitation and injury prevention. During his training he completed an internship at the University of Miami, worked with NFL Europe, and took his passion for rugby to work with various rugby clubs.

James has worked in both NHS and private settings and established Elements Physiotherapy in 2015. He joined Cat Hill Clinic in 2018 and enjoys working within an experienced team and being part of his patients' journeys. He uses a range of techniques, including mobilisation, acupuncture, kinesiotaping and exercise-based rehabilitation, and continues to develop as a clinician by attending regular CPD courses.

In his spare time James enjoys running, the gym and circuit training, coaches the local under-16s rugby team, and takes on Tough Mudder events and the occasional half marathon!

Registered member of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy and the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)

Tracy Ward ITEC IIHHT

Massage Therapist

Tracy qualified in 1997 after training at Enfield College in Anatomy & Physiology and Body Massage Therapy, followed by Level 3 City & Guilds. She went on to train in Aromatherapy (IIHHT), Indian Head Massage (ITEC) and Pregnancy Massage.

Tracy started her massage career in a local beauty salon and has been our resident massage therapist at Cat Hill Clinic for the last 20 years. She takes a holistic approach, carefully adapting and combining techniques to suit the needs of each individual, whether that is deep-tissue Swedish massage, pregnancy massage, gentle aromatherapy or Indian head massage.

Tracy enjoys movies and seeing friends, and is also our longest-standing receptionist!

Registered member of the International Institute of Health and Holistic Therapies

Adam Leighton MBAcC DipAc BSc (Hons) TCM

Acupuncturist

Adam has been practising in Barnet for 28 years, the majority of which was at the Eden Clinic in Bulwer Road. In the summer of 2026 we were delighted to welcome Adam to the Cat Hill Clinic team, and we look forward to a like-minded, holistic approach to providing top-quality patient care.

Adam graduated from Westminster University in 1998 with a degree in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Acupuncture. He has completed a number of postgraduate courses covering scalp and ear acupuncture, cupping, fertility, gynaecological conditions and pregnancy care, Meridian Wave treatment (excellent for pain relief), electro-acupuncture, musculoskeletal pain and TCM. He has also spent time treating patients at the Nanjing Hospital in China.

He has over 30 years' experience of Tai Chi Ch'uan and Chi Kung, and often includes exercises or breathing techniques to aid recovery. Treatment may also incorporate TCM dietary advice, therapeutic massage (using Chinese meridian theory and contemporary teachings), cupping, moxibustion and electro-acupuncture.

Adam enjoys travelling, time with family and continual learning.

Member of the British Acupuncture Council
